Rebuild your smile—one tooth or a whole arch

Missing teeth affect how you look, eat, speak, and feel. Even a single gap shifts your bite, while multiple missing teeth can alter your facial profile entirely. Custom dental prosthetics restore more than function—they give you back the confidence to smile, laugh, and enjoy food without hesitation. From a single porcelain crown to a full-arch implant-supported bridge, every piece is digitally designed and precision-milled for a fit that feels natural from day one.

Why today's dental prosthetics stand out

  • Tailored solutions—whether you need a crown, implant-supported bridge, partial, or full denture, every prosthesis is custom-designed to your arch, bite, and esthetic preferences.
  • CAD/CAM-milled zirconia or layered porcelain deliver lifelike translucency and exceptional strength—far beyond what pressed ceramics offered a decade ago.
  • Restores facial support by replacing missing tooth structure and root form, countering the sunken look that often follows tooth loss.
  • Adhesive-free, screw-retained options provide rock-steady stability without pastes or adhesives—ideal for implant overdentures and full bridges.
  • Immediate-load choices give you same-day fixed teeth in selected cases, so you never spend a day without a complete smile.
  • Biocompatible, metal-free ceramics are perfect for patients with metal sensitivities, offering full-strength function without nickel or base alloys.
  • Long service life—10 to 20+ years with proper home care and routine check-ups, outlasting older acrylic and metal-frame designs.
  • Easy maintenance thanks to glazed, plaque-resistant surfaces and precision margins that resist staining and marginal leakage.

Whether it's a single cracked molar or a full-arch solution, today's prosthetics blend art and engineering to rebuild your smile for the long haul.

Is a dental prosthetic right for you?

Dental prosthetics are designed for anyone with missing or severely damaged teeth who wants a reliable, natural-looking replacement—with or without implant surgery. If tooth loss is affecting your ability to chew comfortably, speak clearly, or smile with confidence, a custom prosthetic can restore function and esthetics, often in fewer visits and at a lower cost than full implant reconstruction.

You're likely a good candidate if you:

  • Are missing one or multiple teeth and have noticed shifting, bite changes, or difficulty chewing certain foods.
  • Prefer a non-surgical or quick-turnaround option—crowns and bridges are typically completed in 1–3 visits without extensive healing time.
  • Have adequate tooth structure or bone support for a fixed restoration, or are comfortable with a removable partial or full denture.
  • Maintain good oral hygiene and commit to regular check-ups to maximize the lifespan of your prosthetic.
  • Seek a cost-effective alternative to implants that still delivers high esthetics and reliable chewing function.
  • Want to restore facial volume and support lips and cheeks for a more youthful profile.
  • Have medical conditions or medications that make surgery risky, but still want to replace missing teeth with a well-fitting prosthetic.

How the process works

1

Comprehensive consultation & 3-D scan

We begin with a full oral exam, digital photos, and a 3-D intra-oral scan (or CBCT for implant-supported cases). This captures your bite, existing teeth, and esthetic goals at up to 20 µm accuracy—no goopy impressions needed.

2

Tooth preparation & precision impressions

For crowns and bridges, teeth are gently contoured under magnification to create space for the restoration. For dentures, we take functional bite registrations and border-molded impressions that capture muscle dynamics for a secure, comfortable fit.

3

Digital design & lab fabrication (7–14 days)

Your case is CAD/CAM designed, then milled from solid zirconia blocks or hand-layered in porcelain. Custom staining and glazing match your natural shade. For dentures, a wax try-in lets you preview the fit and esthetics before final processing.

4

Try-in, fit check & shade verification

We place the prosthesis in your mouth before final cementation. Bite contacts, phonetics, lip support, and shade are verified under both natural and operatory light. Minor adjustments are made chairside to ensure absolute comfort.

5

Final placement & after-care for fixed restorations

Crowns and bridges are cemented or screw-retained using torque-controlled drivers. Final X-rays confirm complete seating and margin seal. You leave with detailed care instructions and a follow-up schedule.

6

Removable prostheses delivery & home-care kit

Full and partial dentures are delivered with a thorough pressure-point check to eliminate sore spots. You receive a home-care kit with cleaning solutions, a storage case, and a maintenance schedule—plus a 48-hour follow-up call to fine-tune anything that needs adjusting.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Crown vs onlay vs filling: how do you decide?

The choice comes down to remaining healthy tooth structure. A filling works when more than half the tooth is intact. An onlay covers one or two cusps when the damage is moderate. A full crown is indicated when more than 50% of the tooth structure is compromised or the tooth has undergone root canal treatment, as it needs circumferential protection against fracture. We evaluate with magnification and digital imaging to recommend the most conservative option that will still last.

Which material fits my case: ceramic, zirconia, hybrid?

For front teeth, layered porcelain provides the best translucency and natural esthetics. For back teeth and bridges, solid zirconia offers the highest fracture resistance—ideal for patients who clench or grind. Hybrid ceramics combine the esthetics of porcelain with the strength of zirconia and work well in the premolar region. We match material to your bite forces, the span being restored, and how much tooth shows when you smile.

How do you make sure the bite feels right after?

We use articulating paper and digital occlusal analysis to map every contact point. Before you leave, you'll bite, chew, and slide in every direction while we watch for high spots. For complex full-mouth cases, we may schedule a longer try-in appointment where you wear the prosthesis temporarily and give feedback over a few days before final cementation. Minor bite adjustments are always included at no extra charge during the first month.

If I need a bridge, how do you protect the supporting teeth?

Supporting teeth (abutments) are prepared as conservatively as possible under magnification. We remove the absolute minimum of enamel needed to seat the bridge while maintaining enough thickness for strength. The bridge is designed with proper connector dimensions and cleansable contours underneath the pontic. A water flosser or floss threader is demonstrated at delivery so you can clean under the bridge daily. With good hygiene, abutment teeth stay healthy for decades.

How many visits, and what can be done same-day?

A single crown or bridge typically takes 2–3 visits: consultation + scan, preparation + temporary, and final placement. Complete dentures usually require 4–5 visits including try-in. For same-day options, we can perform relines, minor adjustments, and repair of existing prostheses on the spot. Select cases qualify for same-day crowns milled in-office with CEREC technology—ask at your consultation whether your case is suitable for a single-visit crown.
