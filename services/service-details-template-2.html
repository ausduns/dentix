Benefits

Why dental implants are the gold standard in tooth replacement

Dental implants are the closest thing to natural teeth. Designed to last for decades, they offer unmatched stability, comfort, and aesthetics. An implant replaces both the root and the visible part of the tooth, helping preserve your jawbone.

What makes dental implants different:

  • Permanent solution for missing or severely damaged teeth
  • Feels and functions like a natural tooth
  • Helps maintain jawbone health and facial structure
  • No adhesives or dietary restrictions
  • High success rate and long-term reliability
Ideal Candidate

Is a dental implant right for you?

Dental implants are suitable for most adults who want a long-term, stable alternative to bridges or removable dentures. They're especially effective if you've lost a tooth due to injury or decay.

Process Overview

How the process works

1. Consultation & Digital Scan
We begin with a thorough assessment and 3D scan to plan your treatment.
2. Operation Procedure
The titanium implant is placed under local anesthesia. Most patients experience little to no discomfort.
3. Healing Period
Over the next 2–3 months, the implant bonds naturally with the jawbone.
4. Crown Placement
Once healed, we attach a custom-made crown for a perfect fit and finish.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Am I a candidate, and what could disqualify me?

Most adults with healthy gums and sufficient bone density are candidates. Uncontrolled diabetes, heavy smoking, or untreated gum disease may temporarily disqualify you until resolved.

Do I need grafting, and how does that change timeline and cost?

If bone volume is insufficient, grafting adds 3-6 months and $350-$1200.

Who does what: placement vs crown, and how is it coordinated?

Our oral surgeon places the implant; our restorative dentist fabricates and attaches the crown – all in-house coordination.
