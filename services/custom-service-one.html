Instant confidence boost for special events

Teeth whitening is the fastest way to upgrade your appearance—whether it's for a wedding, photoshoot, presentation, or just because you're tired of hiding your smile. Our high-potency, pH-balanced gel with LED activation avoids the chalky, uneven look of drugstore strips. Most patients leave 6–8 shades lighter in a single appointment.

Why patients choose professional whitening

  • Dentist-supervised for maximum safety—gums and lips are isolated with custom barriers, and shade is monitored throughout so we hit your target without over-bleaching.
  • Powerful, pH-neutral gel targets coffee, wine, and nicotine stains deep within the enamel while limiting post-treatment sensitivity.
  • Even, natural-looking results—proprietary light diffusion creates uniform whitening edge-to-edge, never blotchy or striped.
  • Take-home trays included with every in-office session—custom-fit trays plus a starter gel kit keep your results fresh before big events.
  • Longevity that fits your lifestyle—results can endure one to three years with good hygiene and occasional touch-ups.
  • Safe for enamel and restorations—won't weaken existing fillings or crowns, and approved for repeated professional use.

A single 90-minute visit can transform your appearance for confident photos and a smile you'll want to show off.

Are you a good whitening candidate?

Professional whitening works for most adults, but a few baseline conditions maximize your results and minimize sensitivity. We'll evaluate all of these during your shade assessment so there are no surprises.

You're an ideal candidate if:

  • Your gums are healthy and you have no active decay—any restorative work should be completed before whitening to avoid irritation or gel seepage.
  • Your stains are extrinsic or mild intrinsic from coffee, tea, red wine, dark sauces, natural aging, or tobacco use.
  • You're not pregnant or breastfeeding—we defer treatment during this period as a precaution, even though the gel has a strong safety profile.
  • Restorations are limited to back teeth—front fillings, veneers, or crowns won't whiten alongside natural enamel. We can replace or shade-match these afterward if needed.
  • You have realistic expectations—most patients achieve 6–8 shades of lift, varying by your natural enamel tone and lifestyle habits.
  • You're committed to after-care—avoiding staining foods and drinks for 48 hours plus using your take-home touch-up trays preserves your results.

Process overview

1

Shade assessment & baseline photos

We use a calibrated shade guide and high-resolution photos to document your starting point. This gives us an objective baseline so you can see the exact improvement at the end of your session.

2

Comprehensive gum isolation & protective barriers

A light-cured resin dam is painted along your gumline to seal and protect soft tissue. Cheek retractors and cotton rolls keep the field dry and comfortable, so only your teeth are exposed to the gel.

3

Triple-cycle gel application + LED activation (3 × 15 min)

Fresh pH-neutral gel is brushed onto each tooth. A cool-temperature LED lamp accelerates peroxide breakdown, releasing oxygen that lifts deep stains from within the enamel. After 15 minutes, the gel is suctioned away and reapplied—three cycles typically deliver ideal brightness without over-sensitizing.

4

Fluoride & potassium-nitrate desensitizing rinse

A remineralizing conditioner seals open dentinal tubules and calms nerve endings. Your teeth leave the chair feeling smooth and strong—not zappy or raw.

5

Post-care kit, shade comparison & maintenance plan

We take your "after" photo with the same shade guide for a side-by-side comparison. You'll receive custom take-home trays, touch-up gel syringes, a 48-hour food-avoidance guide, and a maintenance schedule so your results stay bright for the long run.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does whitening last?

Results typically last one to three years, depending on your diet and habits. Coffee, red wine, tea, and tobacco will gradually re-stain teeth over time. Using your custom take-home trays for a touch-up session every 4–6 months keeps your smile at its brightest. We also recommend a hygiene cleaning beforehand—whitening works best on clean, tartar-free enamel.

Is whitening safe?

Yes, when performed under professional supervision. The gel is pH-neutral and formulated to minimize enamel demineralization. We isolate your gums completely with a light-cured resin barrier, so the gel only touches your teeth. Decades of research support the safety of peroxide-based whitening when administered by a dentist. It does not weaken enamel, existing fillings, or crowns.

Will it cause sensitivity?

Some patients experience mild, temporary sensitivity for 24–48 hours after treatment. This happens because peroxide opens microscopic pores in the enamel, allowing the nerves to sense temperature changes more acutely. We apply a fluoride and potassium-nitrate desensitizing rinse at the end of every session to seal those pores and calm the nerves. Most patients describe it as a mild "awareness" rather than pain, and it resolves quickly.

Does it work on crowns or fillings?

No. Whitening gel only affects natural tooth enamel. Existing crowns, veneers, fillings, and bonding will stay their original shade. If you have visible restorations on front teeth, we recommend whitening first, then replacing the restorations to match your new, brighter shade. We'll map out the right sequence during your consultation so everything matches perfectly.

In-clinic vs take-home trays?

In-clinic whitening delivers immediate, dramatic results in a single 90-minute session with LED acceleration and full gum protection. Take-home trays are a gentler, gradual approach worn for a few hours daily over 1–2 weeks. Many patients choose both: the in-office session for the instant transformation, then custom trays for maintenance. We'll help you decide based on your timeline, budget, and sensitivity level.
