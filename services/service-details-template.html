Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does whitening last?

Results typically last one to three years, depending on your diet and habits. Coffee, red wine, tea, and tobacco will gradually re-stain teeth over time. Using your custom take-home trays for a touch-up session every 4–6 months keeps your smile at its brightest.

Is whitening safe?

Yes, when performed under professional supervision. The gel is pH-neutral and formulated to minimize enamel demineralization.

Will it cause sensitivity?

Some patients experience mild, temporary sensitivity for 24–48 hours after treatment. We apply a fluoride and potassium-nitrate desensitizing rinse at the end of every session to seal pores and calm the nerves.
