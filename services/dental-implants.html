Why dental implants are the gold standard

Dental implants are the closest thing to natural teeth. Designed to last for decades, they offer unmatched stability, comfort, and aesthetics. An implant replaces both the root and the visible part of the tooth, helping preserve your jawbone and prevent future dental issues.

  • Permanent solution for missing teeth
  • Feels and functions like a natural tooth
  • Preserves jawbone and facial structure
  • No adhesives or dietary restrictions
  • High success rate, long-term reliability

Frequently Asked Questions

Am I a candidate, and what could disqualify me?

Most adults with healthy gums and sufficient bone density are candidates. Uncontrolled diabetes, heavy smoking, or untreated gum disease may disqualify.

Do I need grafting, and how does that change timeline and cost?

If bone volume is insufficient, grafting adds 3‑6 months and $350–$1200.

Who does placement vs crown, and how is it coordinated?

Our oral surgeon places the implant; our restorative dentist fabricates and attaches the crown – all in‑house coordination.

How do you reduce infection and peri‑implantitis risk?

Sterile protocols, strict aftercare, and regular maintenance visits.

What will the final tooth look like, and how do I keep it healthy?

The crown is custom‑shaded and shaped. Clean like natural teeth: brush, floss, and regular check‑ups.
