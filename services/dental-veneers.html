Transform your smile in as little as two weeks

Porcelain veneers give you uniform shape, color, and alignment—without braces or repeated whitening treatments. Custom-milled shells are bonded to the front surface of each tooth with microscopic precision, creating a natural, harmonious result that looks like you were born with it.

Why patients love veneers

  • Ultra-conservative preparation—enamel removal is limited to just 0.3–0.5 mm, preserving the vast majority of healthy tooth structure.
  • Digital shade-matching & smile design uses intraoral scanners and CAD/CAM software so your veneers blend seamlessly with surrounding teeth.
  • Stain-resistant zirconia-reinforced porcelain actually outperforms natural enamel against coffee, tea, red wine, and nicotine.
  • One solution for multiple concerns—gaps, chips, intrinsic stains, and mild crowding can all be addressed simultaneously.
  • 10–15+ year longevity with routine hygiene and a custom night guard to protect your investment.
  • Immediate confidence boost that patients report in photos, presentations, and social settings from day one.

In just two visits, you can have a smile that's magazine-ready yet completely you.

Are veneers right for you?

Veneers are ideal for adults with good oral health who want a dramatic esthetic upgrade without braces or extended whitening regimens. Because the porcelain bonds directly to enamel, you need enough healthy tooth structure for a strong, lasting adhesion.

You're likely an excellent candidate if you have:

  • Persistent discoloration that hasn't responded to professional whitening—including tetracycline stains, fluorosis, or deep intrinsic darkening.
  • Minor spacing, crowding, or chipped edges that can be corrected quickly and conservatively without orthodontics.
  • Sufficient enamel thickness confirmed by trans-illumination during your exam—essential for a strong bond.
  • Healthy gums and no active decay—any periodontal issues or cavities should be treated before veneer placement.
  • A stable bite with controlled bruxism—night guards can protect veneers, but severe unmanaged grinding is a contraindication.
  • Desire for a low-maintenance, permanent solution that lasts 10–15+ years with proper care.
  • Realistic expectations—veneers transform the visible front surfaces but don't whiten existing crowns or fillings on back teeth.

Not recommended for patients with untreated gum disease, severe enamel erosion, or active, uncontrolled clenching and grinding.

How the process works

1

Digital Smile Design & chairside mock-up

High-resolution scans and photos are loaded into our smile-design software. Ideal proportions are mapped to your facial features, and a resin mock-up is placed directly on your teeth so you can preview the final look before a single millimeter of enamel is touched.

2

Minimal-prep contouring & precision impressions

Only 0.3–0.5 mm of enamel is gently reshaped—often without anesthesia. An intraoral scanner captures a 3-D digital model accurate to 20 microns, eliminating the need for traditional goopy impressions.

3

Custom temporary veneers

Tooth-colored provisionals protect your prepared enamel while your final veneers are being fabricated. You'll wear them for 1–2 weeks, giving you time to test the shape and provide feedback on length, contour, and edge detail that guides the lab's final work.

4

Bonding appointment

Your porcelain veneers are tried in with a clear glycerin gel so you can evaluate shade and fit under natural light. Once you approve, each tooth is etched, primed, and bonded using a light-cured adhesive system that creates a bond stronger than traditional filling material.

5

Final polish, bite balancing & maintenance plan

Your bite is fine-tuned until every contact feels natural, margins are polished to a glass finish, and after-photos are taken. You'll leave with a home-care kit, 48-hour dietary tips for the bonding to fully cure, and a custom night guard to protect your new smile for the long haul.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need veneers, or would whitening and bonding be enough?

It depends on what you're trying to fix. Whitening only addresses color, and composite bonding works well for small chips or gaps. Veneers are the right choice when you want to change shape, size, alignment, and color all at once—or when stains are intrinsic and won't respond to bleaching. During your smile design consultation, we'll show you what each option would look like so you can compare before committing.

How much tooth reduction do you typically do?

We limit preparation to 0.3–0.5 mm—roughly the thickness of a contact lens. Many cases can be done with little to no anesthesia because we stay within the enamel layer. Some patients even qualify for "no-prep" or "minimal-prep" veneers where reduction is limited to superficial texturing. We'll measure your enamel thickness during the exam and recommend the most conservative approach that still delivers the result you want.

How do you avoid the "too white / too big" look?

This comes down to three things: proportion, shade, and your input. We digitally map your facial midline, lip line, and smile arc so the veneers complement your features—not a one-size-fits-all template. Shade is matched to your skin tone, eye color, and the whites of your eyes for natural brightness. And the temporary try-in phase lets you live with the shape for a week or two and give feedback before the lab finalizes anything. You're never guessing what the outcome will be.

Can I preview the result before anything permanent?

Yes—two ways. First, we do a digital smile simulation on screen during your consultation so you can see the projected outcome. Second, we fabricate a resin mock-up directly on your teeth at the chair. You'll see it in a hand mirror and can even take photos home. The mock-up is removable and no enamel is touched until you're fully satisfied with the design direction.

What happens if one chips later or gums change?

Minor chips can often be polished or repaired with composite resin in a single visit. If a veneer is significantly damaged, a replacement can be fabricated to match the existing set. As for gum changes—if recession occurs over the years, the exposed margin can sometimes be covered with a small bonding addition, or the veneer can be replaced. These are rare occurrences with proper home care and regular check-ups, and we'll catch any early signs during your routine hygiene visits.
