The foundation of lifelong oral health

A bright smile starts with healthy gums. Routine hygiene appointments do more than polish away coffee stains—they remove the bacterial biofilm that fuels gum disease, cavities, and even systemic conditions like cardiovascular inflammation. Our hygienists combine state-of-the-art instrumentation with personalized coaching, so every visit leaves your mouth cleaner and your daily routine smarter.

Why professional hygiene matters

  • Ultrasonic and hand scaling reach below-gum deposits that toothbrushes and floss can't touch, stopping early periodontal pockets in their tracks.
  • Air-polishing and high-gloss cups lift surface stains from coffee, tea, and wine, revealing your natural enamel brilliance.
  • Personalized home-care coaching—from floss-threaders around bridges to water-flosser settings—empowers you to maintain results between visits.
  • Early detection of cavities, cracked fillings, or oral lesions allows for quick, minimally invasive treatment and peace of mind.
  • Fluoride varnish or sealants remineralize enamel and protect deep grooves, drastically reducing future decay risk.
  • Oral–systemic screening links gum health to heart disease, diabetes, and pregnancy outcomes; we track key indicators and coordinate with your physician when needed.
  • Fresh-breath protocols neutralize odor-causing bacteria, giving you lasting confidence in close conversations.

Regular preventive care is the simplest, most cost-effective way to keep your smile—and overall health—at its best.

Is it time for a cleaning?

Routine cleanings every six months keep plaque and tartar in check for virtually everyone—but certain groups benefit even more from staying on a strict hygiene schedule (or visiting every 3–4 months). If you fall into any of the categories below, booking a professional cleaning now can prevent gum disease, cavities, and costly dental work down the road.

You’re an excellent candidate if you:

  • Smoke or vape – nicotine reduces blood flow to the gums, masking early inflammation.
  • Wear braces, aligners, or fixed retainers – brackets and wires trap food debris that’s hard to remove at home.
  • Have a history of gum issues – previous gingivitis or periodontitis makes you more susceptible to future flare-ups.
  • Manage diabetes or heart disease – systemic conditions can worsen oral health (and vice versa).
  • Experience dry mouth from medications or menopause—reduced saliva raises cavity risk.
  • Notice persistent bad breath or a fuzzy film on your teeth even after brushing.
  • Skipped your last checkup or can’t recall your last professional polish—if it’s been over six months, you’re due.

A quick, comfortable hygiene visit now can save you discomfort—and money—later.

Process overview

1

Comprehensive periodontal charting & screening

We measure pocket depths, check gum attachment levels, and record any inflammation or bleeding points to create a detailed baseline of your gum health.

2

Ultrasonic + precision hand scaling

A gentle ultrasonic scaler breaks up heavy tartar both above and below the gumline, followed by fine hand instruments that remove remaining deposits for a glass-smooth root surface.

3

Air-polish, flossing & stain removal

A pressurized stream of fine powder and water lifts surface stains from tea, coffee, or wine. We complete the polish with flossing to sweep away residual debris between teeth.

4

Targeted fluoride varnish or sealants (optional)

High-strength fluoride varnish is painted onto enamel to remineralize and strengthen weak spots. Deep grooves on molars can be sealed the same visit to block future decay.

5

Personalized oral-health coaching & recall scheduling

We review brushing technique, demonstrate any specialty tools you may need, and set a recall interval (3, 4, or 6 months) tailored to your risk profile—so you leave with a clear plan and a healthier smile.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I get a cleaning?

Most adults and children benefit from a professional cleaning every six months. If you have gum disease, diabetes, or a history of cavities, we may recommend a 3- or 4-month recall interval to stay ahead of bacterial buildup before it causes damage.

What happens during a hygiene visit?

Your visit begins with a gum-health screening where we measure pocket depths and check for inflammation. Next, we use an ultrasonic scaler and fine hand instruments to remove tartar above and below the gumline, followed by air-polishing to lift surface stains. We finish with flossing, an optional fluoride varnish, and personalized home-care coaching so you leave with a clear plan.

Does cleaning hurt?

For most patients, a routine cleaning causes little to no discomfort. If your gums are inflamed or you have sensitive teeth, we can use a topical numbing gel to keep you comfortable. You may feel light scraping or vibration from the scaler, but it shouldn’t be painful. Mild gum soreness afterward typically resolves within 24 hours.

Do I need X-rays every time?

No. Bitewing X-rays are typically taken every 12–24 months based on your caries risk. During a hygiene visit, we only recommend them if it’s been over a year since your last set or if we see a reason to check for hidden decay between teeth. Your hygienist will discuss this before taking any images.

Can a cleaning whiten my teeth?

A professional cleaning removes surface stains from coffee, tea, and wine, so your teeth will look brighter afterward. However, it doesn’t change your natural enamel shade the way a whitening treatment does. If you’re looking for a noticeably whiter smile, we can pair your cleaning with an in-office or take-home whitening treatment—ask your hygienist for details.
