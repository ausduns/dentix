Benefits

The foundation of lifelong oral health

A bright smile starts with healthy gums. Routine hygiene appointments do more than polish away coffee stains — they prevent the kind of silent gum disease that leads to tooth loss and systemic inflammation.

Ultrasonic and hand scaling reach below-gum deposits that toothbrushes and floss can't touch, stopping bacteria before it hardens into calculus.
Air-polishing and high-gloss cups lift surface stains from coffee, tea, and wine, revealing your natural shade without peroxide.
Personalized home-care coaching — from floss-threaders around bridges to water-flosser settings — empowers you between visits.
Early detection of cavities, cracked fillings, or oral lesions allows for quick, minimally invasive fixes.
Oral–systemic screening links gum health to heart disease, diabetes, and pregnancy outcomes; we track changes visit to visit.

Ideal Candidate

Everyone. The American Dental Association recommends a hygiene visit every 6 months — more frequently if you have active periodontal disease, diabetes, or are a smoker. Children benefit from early visits that teach brushing habits and catch developing bite issues. Seniors with bridges, implants, or dry-mouth from medications need specialized care. If it's been over a year since your last cleaning, there's no judgment — only a fresh start.

Process Overview

A hygiene visit at Dentix takes about an hour. We start with a gum-health assessment — pocket-depth measurements around every tooth — because healthy gums are the foundation for everything else. Next comes ultrasonic scaling to break up calculus, followed by hand instruments for fine contouring around crowns and bridges. After air-polishing and a high-gloss buff, we apply fluoride varnish or sealants if needed. You leave with a mirror-check, a personalized home-care plan, and your next visit already on the calendar.
