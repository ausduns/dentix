How We Work

What Sets Us Apart

No treatment starts without a full picture. Every patient goes through 3D CBCT scans, intraoral imaging, and a clinical evaluation by the relevant specialists before anything is recommended. You'll understand your options and the reasoning behind each one before you commit.

  • ✓Full 3D cone beam imaging on every case
  • ✓Multi-specialist review before any plan is presented
  • ✓Digital smile previews so you see the result upfront

Your case isn't passed between disconnected offices through referral slips. It's planned in person, adjusted in real time, and delivered by specialists who share the same hallway. When something needs to change mid-treatment, the decision is made the same day.

  • ✓No external referrals for core procedures
  • ✓Shared treatment plans across all specialists
  • ✓Faster timelines because coordination happens internally

You'll receive a detailed treatment plan with clear pricing before any procedure begins. If a case requires bone grafting, multiple implants, or a phased approach — every stage and every fee is laid out from day one.

  • ✓Itemized plans with full cost breakdowns
  • ✓Multi-specialist review before any plan is presented
  • ✓No hidden fees, no scope surprises

This isn't a general practice that added an implant chair. The clinic was designed from the ground up for advanced restorative and surgical work — dedicated surgical suites, a digital prosthetics lab for in-house fabrication, and full sedation capabilities.

  • ✓Dedicated surgical suites with laminar airflow
  • ✓In-house digital lab for same-day prosthetic work
  • ✓Full IV sedation for surgical procedures
