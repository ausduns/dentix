Background

Dr. Tracy Willow is the founder and clinical director of Dentix. She built the practice around a single idea: that complex restorative dentistry should be delivered by a focused team of specialists working together under one roof.

She completed her Doctor of Dental Surgery at the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine, followed by advanced surgical training in implantology and prosthetic rehabilitation.
